    Amazon Kindle Delivery Setup

    Seamlessly deliver articles wirelessly to your Kindle e-reader using Amazon's free Send to Kindle service.

    Step 1: Find your Kindle email address

    Go to Amazon > Manage Your Content and Devices > Preferences > Personal Document Settings. You will see your Kindle email address (ends with @kindle.com).

    Step 2: Approve the Sender Email in Amazon

    In the same Amazon settings page, look for Approved Personal Document E-mail List and add your sender email address (e.g., your personal Gmail or custom email address).

    Step 3: Save your credentials below

    Enter your Kindle email address and optional SMTP details for automatic 1-click delivery.
